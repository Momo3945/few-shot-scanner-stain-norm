\textbf{Why the learned baselines win.} ParamNet and StainNet apply a colour map to the input pixels, and
StainGAN's CycleGAN generator keeps a wide feature map (a quarter of the resolution, hundreds of
channels); none of them passes the image through a 48$\times$ compressing four-channel latent. Every diffusion output is resynthesised by the VAE, which alone limits SSIM to
0.576--0.646 on Aperio crops (Section~\ref{sec:vae}). F3 fusion in effect turns the diffusion model back
into a colour map applied to the source. With 50 pairs, the strong image prior
of a diffusion model does not compensate for the information it loses in its own bottleneck.

\textbf{Colour metrics.} LAB Wasserstein ignores layout, so histogram matching wins it on A06 by
construction while scoring worse than doing nothing on typical slides; the windowed variant and per-slide
reporting prevent reading that as a real gain. Pixelwise $\Delta E_{2000}$ tracks SSIM almost rank for
rank, so it is not an independent colour check.

\textbf{Cost.} On one RTX~3090 (Table~\ref{tab:app-speed}) the final pipeline takes 6.27\,s per 512\,px
crop, against 2--42\,ms for every baseline. A 100{,}000$\times$100{,}000 pixel slide holds about 38{,}000
such crops, so it needs about 66 GPU-hours against minutes for the baselines.

\textbf{Limitations.} All paired training data come from one slide pair (A03/H03), so slide-level variation is
not represented in training. Our LoRA and ControlNet were trained once, so their training-seed variation is unmeasured. Structural
safety rests on one segmenter (HoVer-Net) and one colon dataset (Lizard). No clinical-utility claim is made: every atypia
classifier we built failed its own pre-specified gate (Appendix~\ref{app:clinical}). The decoder smoothing analysis
rests on two inspected crops.

\textbf{Planned but not run.} No result is claimed for the mitosis-detection arm (it needs a separate
detector), the SD\,3.5 structural-ControlNet and Turbo checks, a full-VAE fine-tune with the LoRA and
ControlNet retrained (only the anchored version was run), or a PixCell source-conditioning branch. The
TCGA-BRCA secondary colour target was not used, and the planned state-of-the-art baseline, the
attention-residual method~\cite{madusanka2025attentionresidual}, was not reproduced, so it is cited but not
compared.
