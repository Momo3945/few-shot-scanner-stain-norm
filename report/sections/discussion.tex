\textbf{Why the learned baselines win.} ParamNet applies a colour map to the input pixels, and
StainGAN's CycleGAN generator keeps a wide feature map; neither passes the image through a 48$\times$ compressing four-channel latent. Every diffusion output is resynthesised by the VAE, which alone limits SSIM to
0.576--0.646 on Aperio crops (Section~\ref{sec:vae}). F3 fusion in effect turns the diffusion model back
into a colour map applied to the source. With 50 pairs, the strong image prior
of a diffusion model does not compensate for the information it loses in its own bottleneck.

\textbf{Cost.} On one RTX~3090 (Table~\ref{tab:app-speed}) the final pipeline takes 6.27\,s per 512\,px
crop, against 2--42\,ms for every baseline. A 100{,}000$\times$100{,}000 pixel slide (about 38{,}000
crops) needs about 66 GPU-hours, against minutes for the baselines.

\textbf{Limitations.} All paired training data come from one slide pair (A03/H03), so slide-level variation is
not represented in training. Our LoRA and ControlNet were trained once, so their training-seed variation is unmeasured. Structural
safety rests on one segmenter (HoVer-Net) and one colon dataset (Lizard). No clinical-utility claim is made: every atypia
classifier we built failed its own pre-specified gate (Appendix~\ref{app:clinical}). The decoder smoothing analysis
rests on two inspected crops.

\textbf{Not run.} The mitosis-detection arm, an SD\,1.5 LoRA-weight sweep under LCM, the SD\,3.5 structural-ControlNet and Turbo checks, an
unanchored full-VAE fine-tune, a PixCell source-conditioning branch (a new build for a diffusion transformer, outside the proposal's scope) and the TCGA-BRCA colour target. Neither
the attention-residual method~\cite{madusanka2025attentionresidual} nor the diffusion normalisers of
Section~\ref{sec:related} were reproduced, so they are cited but not compared.
